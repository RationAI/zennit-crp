| Analyzing Multi-Head Self-Attention: Specialized Heads Do the Heavy Lifting, the Rest Can Be Pruned (Voita et al.) | ACL (57th Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics) — peer-reviewed conference | 2019 | Scores each encoder head's importance and confidence with layer-wise relevance propagation and prunes heads via stochastic gates with a differentiable L0 penalty, then manually groups the surviving specialist heads into linguistically interpretable roles (positional, syntactic/dependency-like, and rare-word-tracking heads). |
| Are Sixteen Heads Really Better than One? (Michel et al.) | NeurIPS (33rd Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems) — peer-reviewed conference | 2019 | Characterises heads by test-time ablation: uses a gradient-based proxy for the sensitivity of the loss to masking each head to order heads, then greedily prunes them while measuring BLEU/perplexity (and downstream-task scores), showing most heads are redundant and only a small subset is task-critical. |
| What Does BERT Look At? An Analysis of BERT's Attention (Clark et al.) | BlackboxNLP 2019 (ACL workshop on Analyzing and Interpreting Neural Networks for NLP) — peer-reviewed workshop proceedings | 2019 | Directly analyses per-head attention distributions — categorising surface patterns (attending to separator tokens, fixed relative positions, broad sentence-level averaging) and quantitatively probing how well each head's attention aligns with gold linguistic annotations such as dependency relations (e.g. direct objects, determiners) and coreference. |
| Analyzing Transformers in Embedding Space (Dar et al.) | ACL 2023 (61st Annual Meeting of the ACL, Long Papers) — peer-reviewed conference | 2023 | Uses an input-independent, parameter-based framework that projects each component (including attention heads' OV circuits) directly into the vocabulary embedding space, reading each head as a fixed update that promotes or suppresses specific vocabulary items/tokens. |
| Interpreting CLIP's Image Representation via Text-Based Decomposition (Gandelsman et al.) | ICLR (International Conference on Learning Representations) — peer-reviewed conference | 2024 | Decomposes CLIP's image representation into a sum of individual attention-head outputs and characterises each head by automatically finding a sparse set of text directions spanning its output space in CLIP's joint text-image space, yielding property-specific head roles (e.g. location, shape, colour) that are validated causally via head ablations. |
| Decomposing and Interpreting Image Representations via Text in ViTs Beyond CLIP (Balasubramanian et al.) | NeurIPS 2024 (Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 37) — peer-reviewed conference | 2024 | Extends head/component decomposition to arbitrary ViTs (DINOv2, DeiT, etc.) by linearly mapping each head's residual contribution into CLIP's text space for text-based interpretation, and ranks heads with a contrastive importance-scoring function that measures how causally important each component is for a given visual property. |
| Attention Heads of Large Language Models: A Survey (Zheng et al.) | arXiv preprint (arXiv:2409.03752) — NOT peer-reviewed | 2024 | Surveys the mechanistic-interpretability literature on attention heads and organises their known functions within a four-stage reasoning framework (Knowledge Recalling, In-Context Identification, Latent Reasoning, Expression Preparation), also reviewing the experimental methods used to measure individual head roles. |
