Related work: LRP × Sparse Autoencoders

Literature scan for the SAE-basis CRP project — does decomposing a ViT prediction's relevance onto a trained SAE dictionary (output→SAE, with conservation, evaluated by concept-flipping/AOPC vs the axis-aligned basis) already exist? · compiled 2026-06-09.

Bottom line. The exact thing we do — initialise relevance at the output class logit and use LRP/CRP to decompose it onto a trained SAE latent basis (output→SAE), with a relevance-conservation check and a concept-flipping / AOPC faithfulness comparison against the axis-aligned embedding-dimension basis, on a ViT — was not found in any paper. The closest works each diverge on a load-bearing axis: CaFE runs LRP in the opposite direction (SAE feature→input); ClassifSAE goes decision→SAE-concepts but by ablation, not conservative relevance propagation; “Sparse but not Simpler” measures SAE-features and attribution as two separate axes; Interpreto has no LRP and keeps its SAE and attribution modules unlinked (fusing them is listed as future work). Our contribution is intact — but it must be positioned explicitly against these.

Our approach (the baseline for every “difference” below)

Train an SAE on ViT activations at a probe site (attention proj_drop output or residual stream); splice it in as a reconstruction pass-through exposing the latent codes f as a recordable layer; initialise relevance at the output class logit and use LRP/CRP rules to decompose that output relevance onto the SAE latents (conditionally maskable per latent); evaluate with concept-flipping / AOPC (most- vs least-relevant ablation) — is the SAE basis more faithful than axis-aligned embedding dims at the same site? — plus a conservation check Σ R(latenti) ≈ R(probe).

Closest prior work (read in full)

CaFE — Causal Interpretation of Sparse Autoencoder Features in Vision CLOSEST · same toolkit

Han, Kim, Kwak (Seoul National Univ.), Aug 2025 · arXiv:2509.00749 · eXCV Workshop @ ICCV 2025 (workshop, not main-track)

What it does. For a single SAE feature on CLIP-ViT-L/14, it finds the “effective receptive field” — the input patches that causally drive the feature — by backpropagating relevance from that SAE latent through the encoder and the ViT down to input pixels, using AttnLRP. Motivated by the fact that high-activation patches are merely correlational under global attention.

Why related. It is the one paper that combines SAE + AttnLRP on a ViT — same building blocks as us, and shares the “raw activation is not causal, you need relevance” motivation.

Key differences.

  • Opposite direction: feature→input (latent is the relevance source); ours is output-logit→SAE (latents are the sinks).
  • Target: a single SAE latent vs the class logit.
  • No conservation check; no axis-aligned-vs-SAE basis comparison; no concept-flipping/AOPC. Only an insertion test on the feature activation.

Take from it. Primary citation — must be the paper we differentiate against (direction + target + conservation + basis comparison). Borrowable: their insertion-test-on-feature as a complementary feature-level faithfulness probe; the “non-local feature” phenomenon as motivation.

ClassifSAE — Unveiling Decision-Making in LLMs … with Sparse Autoencoders CLOSEST · same direction

Le Bail, Dentan, Buscaldi, Vanier, Jun 2025 (rev. Feb 2026) · arXiv:2506.23951 · arXiv preprint only

What it does. Trains a classification-aware SAE on LLM residual-stream activations (joint reconstruction + a classifier head on a latent subset + an activation-rate sparsity loss), then measures each concept's influence on the decision by ablation (Δaccuracy, label-flip rate, total-variation distance), global and conditional.

Why related. Same direction and goal as us — attribute a classification decision onto SAE concepts, framed as faithfulness to the model's prediction. The nearest work on intent.

Key differences.

  • Mechanism: black-box ablation (perturb one latent, watch the output) — causal but heuristic, one concept at a time, off-distribution risk. Ours is a single conservative LRP/CRP backward pass giving an additive relevance vector over all latents at once.
  • No conservation; no ranked MoRF/LeRF AOPC curve (only per-concept effects).
  • Modality: LLM text classification, not ViT.
  • They modify the SAE (classifier head); ours is attribution-side, SAE-agnostic.

Take from it. Cite as the closest “decision→SAE-concept” work and contrast mechanism (ablation vs conservative relevance). Borrowable: their ablation metrics (ΔAcc / flip / TVD) as an independent causal cross-check of our relevance ranking; the conditional-vs-global evaluation split for sparse features.

Sparse but not Simpler — Multi-Level Interpretability Analysis of ViTs CLOSE · same ingredients, separate

Siyu Zhang (UT Austin), Mar 2026 · arXiv:2603.15919 · arXiv preprint only

What it does. Asks whether weight pruning makes ViTs more interpretable. Introduces IMPACT, a 4-level harness (neurons, layer/BatchTopK SAE, circuits, model-level attribution via Chefer transformer-attribution with insertion/deletion). Negative result: sparsity shrinks circuits but gives no systematic interpretability gain.

Why related. Combines SAEs + attribution faithfulness on the same ViT (DeiT-III B/16) and uses an insertion/deletion protocol close in spirit to our AOPC.

Key differences.

  • SAE and attribution are separate measurement axes — relevance is never propagated through the SAE; attribution lands only on input pixels.
  • Independent variable = pruning level, not the representation basis. No SAE-vs-embedding-dim faithfulness comparison; no ranked SAE-feature flipping.

Take from it. Cite for the “sparse ≠ more interpretable/faithful” framing and for BatchTopK-SAE-on-DeiT-III precedent. Borrowable: their insertion/deletion protocol and four automated SAE-quality metrics as complementary measures. We close the loop they leave open — attribute output relevance through the SAE onto its latents.

Adjacent / methodological

Interpreto — An Explainability Library for Transformers ADJACENT · tooling

Poché et al. (IRT Saint Exupéry / IRIT / CentraleSupélec), Dec 2025, ACL 2026 demo · arXiv:2512.09730 · code

Why related & difference. A library bundling attribution methods and a concept pipeline (incl. several SAE variants) for transformers. But it has no LRP/AttnLRP (gradient/perturbation only), estimates concept importance by concept-to-output gradients (no conservation), keeps the attribution and SAE modules unlinked (“link the two modules” is explicit future work), and is NLP-only (ViT on the roadmap).

Take from it. Evidence that combining propagation-attribution with an SAE basis is an acknowledged gap in current tooling — supports our novelty. Cite as the packaged- concept-pipeline reference and contrast (gradient importance, no conservation, NLP).

Unlocking Layer-wise Relevance Propagation for Autoencoders ADJACENT · actionable for our bias fix

Kobayashi, Khasanova, Schneuwly, Schmidt, Casserini (Oracle Labs), Mar 2023 · arXiv:2303.11734 · arXiv preprint only

Why related. LRP through an autoencoder: a Deep-Taylor-derived rule attributes reconstruction error back to input features (for anomaly detection), with a conservation guarantee at the loss layer. Generic AEs (not sparse), but the only paper on “LRP + autoencoder + conservation.”

Take from it — directly useful. It does not solve our decoder-bias absorption (it uses z+, which simply drops biases; no bottleneck rule), but its Deep-Taylor root-point recipe is the principled template for the fix we need: choose a decoder root point that separates the constant mean/bias term from the latent contribution (e.g. root at the mean activation so the residual z−z˜ carries the relevance), instead of letting the γ-rule denominator hand ~98% to the bias. Cite + use as the methodological basis for a bias-aware decoder rule.

Foundations & context (cite, no overlap)

  • CRP — Achtibat et al., From attribution maps to human-understandable explanations through Concept Relevance Propagation, Nat. Mach. Intell. 2023. arXiv:2206.03208. Our foundation — the output→concept relevance method we extend from an axis-aligned to an SAE basis.
  • AttnLRP — Achtibat et al., ICML 2024. arXiv:2402.05602. The transformer LRP rules we build on; also the attribution backbone CaFE uses.
  • Sparse Feature Circuits — Marks et al., ICLR 2025. arXiv:2403.19647. SAE-feature circuits in LMs via gradient attribution-patching — SAE attribution but gradient-based, LM, no conservation.
  • Sparse Explanations via Pruned LRP — Hatefi et al., ECML PKDD 2024 (Springer LNCS 14944, 336–351). arXiv:2404.14271. Sparsity within LRP (prune the relevance flow) — not an SAE basis; tangential.
  • TopK SAEs / Scaling SAEs — Gao et al., 2024. arXiv:2406.04093. SAE training recipe (drop-in if L1 shrinkage hurts reconstruction).
  • SAEs on ViT activations (interpretable but no LRP) — saev, 2502.06755; Universal SAEs, 2502.03714; monosemantic SAEs in VLMs, 2504.02821 (NeurIPS 2025). Precedent that SAEs on ViT residual streams yield interpretable features — the basis we attribute onto.
  • Superposition / Monosemanticity — Elhage et al. 2022 (2209.10652); Bricken et al. 2023 (Towards Monosemanticity). The motivation that features are non-axis-aligned — why an SAE basis should beat embedding dims.
  • LLEXICORP — CRP + multimodal-LLM captioning, 2025. arXiv:2511.02720. Shows CRP being extended, but to LLM labelling of concepts, not an SAE basis.

What this means for us

  • Novelty holds. Frame the contribution precisely as: conservative output→SAE- basis relevance decomposition (the CRP move) on a ViT, with a concept-flipping/AOPC faithfulness comparison against the axis-aligned basis at the same probe site. Differentiate explicitly from CaFE (direction), ClassifSAE (ablation vs conservation; LLM), and Sparse-but-not-Simpler (separate axes).
  • Fix the conservation story before claiming Q2. The decoder-bias absorption is the one technical weakness; 2303.11734's root-point recipe is the principled route to a bias-aware decoder rule.
  • Strengthen evaluation by borrowing: add an independent ablation cross-check (ClassifSAE's ΔAcc/flip/TVD) and report insertion/deletion alongside AOPC (Sparse-but-not-Simpler, CaFE) so reviewers see convergent faithfulness evidence.
